The Idea

You speak from the first session — not after months of grammar.

The Ulpan-Or method teaches the way a first language is learned: through dialogue, image, and repetition — not worksheets. Here's roughly what week one sounds like:

שָׁלוֹם! מָה הַשֵּׁם שֶׁלְּךָ?
SHALOM! MA HA-SHEM SHEL-CHA?
Hello! What's your name?
שְׁמִי דָּוִד. נָעִים מְאֹד!
SHMI DAVID. NA'IM ME'OD!
My name is David. Nice to meet you!

Who It's For

Two levels, so it's important: tell us which one is you.

Track A

Absolute Beginner

Never studied Hebrew at all. You'll start with first sounds and words — zero prior Hebrew needed.

Track B

Novice Low

You know a few words or letters — maybe from Hebrew school years ago — and you're ready to build your first real sentences.
